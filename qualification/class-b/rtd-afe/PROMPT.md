# Prompt

Design a complete, manufacturable 4-layer PT100 4-wire RTD analog front end PCB that includes an RC input filter with 1k 0.1 % series resistors, a 100 nF differential capacitor and 10 nF common-mode capacitors, a TI ADS124S08 24-bit delta-sigma ADC with 1 mA IDAC excitation and a 1k bias resistor, a TI REF5025 2.5 V reference placed close to the ADC, a ferrite-filtered 3.3 V analog rail, an RP2350 microcontroller over SPI with W25Q128 QSPI flash and a 12 MHz crystal, a USB-C readout port with ESD protection and an LP5907 3.3 V LDO, an SWD header, M2.5 mounting holes and one GND plane on all four layers, with analog parts on one side and digital parts on the other, using the Synth MCP server (`synth mcp`) to export the KiCad project and fab package.
